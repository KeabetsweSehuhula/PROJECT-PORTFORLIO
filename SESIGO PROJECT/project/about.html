About Sesigo Saccos

Building financial futures together for over 15 years

Our Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and innovative financial services that empower our members to achieve their financial goals and contribute to the economic development of Botswana.

Our Vision

To be the leading financial cooperative in Botswana, known for excellence, trust, and community impact, while promoting financial inclusion and sustainable development.

Our Values

  • Integrity & Transparency
  • Community First
  • Financial Inclusion
  • Innovation & Excellence
  • Sustainable Growth

Our Journey

From humble beginnings to serving thousands of members across Botswana

2010

Foundation

Sesigo Saccos was established by a group of 50 founding members with a vision to provide financial services to underserved communities in Botswana.

2013

First Expansion

Reached 500 members and launched our first loan products, focusing on small business development and personal financial growth.

2016

Digital Innovation

Introduced online banking services and mobile money integration, making financial services more accessible to rural communities.

2019

Community Impact

Launched financial education programs and crossed 2,000 active members, establishing ourselves as a trusted community partner.

2022

Recognition

Received multiple awards for excellence in cooperative banking and community development initiatives.

2025

Future Ready

Now serving 5,000+ members with innovative digital solutions and expanded service offerings across Botswana.
