Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a member of Sesigo Saccos?

To become a member, you need to complete our membership application form, provide required documents (ID, proof of income, proof of residence), and make an initial deposit of P100. The process typically takes 2-3 business days.

What are the loan requirements?

Loan requirements include: being a member for at least 3 months, having a steady income, providing pay slips for the last 3 months, and having guarantors for larger loans. Specific requirements vary by loan type.

How can I access my account online?

You can access your account through our mobile app or website using your member number and PIN. If you haven't registered for online banking, visit our branch or contact customer service to set it up.

What happens if I miss a loan payment?

We understand that financial difficulties can arise. Contact us immediately if you anticipate missing a payment. We offer flexible payment arrangements and can discuss restructuring options to help you stay on track.
