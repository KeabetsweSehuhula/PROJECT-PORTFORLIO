How to Join

Simple steps to become a Sesigo Saccos member

1

Complete Application

Fill out our online membership application form with your personal and employment details.

2

Submit Documents

Provide required documents: copy of ID, proof of income, and proof of residence.

3

Initial Deposit

Make your initial deposit of P100 to activate your membership and start saving.

4

Welcome Package

Receive your member card, banking details, and access to all Sesigo Saccos services.

Membership Requirements

Must be 18 years or older
Valid Botswana National ID or Work Permit
Proof of regular income (salary slips or bank statements)
Proof of residence (utility bill or rental agreement)
Initial membership deposit of P100
Agree to abide by SACCO constitution and bylaws
