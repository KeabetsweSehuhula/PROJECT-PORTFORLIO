Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What are your operating hours?

Our main branch is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Our call center operates Monday to Friday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays and public holidays.

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We strive to respond to all inquiries promptly. Email inquiries are typically answered within 24 hours during business days. Phone calls are answered immediately during operating hours. Live chat responses are usually within 2-3 minutes.

Can I visit your office without an appointment?

Yes, you can visit our office during business hours without an appointment. However, for loan applications, membership consultations, or complex inquiries, we recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure you receive dedicated time with our specialists.

What documents should I bring for my visit?

For membership applications, bring your ID, proof of income, and proof of residence. For loan applications, additional documents may be required including pay slips, bank statements, and collateral information. Contact us beforehand to confirm specific requirements for your situation.

Do you offer services in languages other than English?

Yes, our staff is multilingual and can assist you in Setswana, English, and other local languages. If you prefer to communicate in a specific language, please let us know when you contact us or visit our office.

How can I file a complaint or provide feedback?

We welcome your feedback and take all complaints seriously. You can file a complaint through our contact form, by calling our customer service line, or by visiting our office in person. We will investigate all complaints promptly and provide you with a resolution within 5 business days.
